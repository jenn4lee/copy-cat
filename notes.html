Copy Cat: Miffy and the balloon

Annotated analysis

  1. Balloon scale: The balloon is almost the same width as Miffy's head. Its bright orange color makes it stand out against the yellow background.
  2. String connection: The thin string connects the balloon to Miffy's hand, bringing the two parts of the image together.
  3. Balance: Miffy is positioned toward the bottom left while the balloon is toward the top right, creating a balanced composition.
  4. Alignment: The top of the balloon is almost aligned with the tops of Miffy's ears.
  5. Facial features: The eyes and mouth are small and placed lower on the face, keeping the design simple.
  6. Negative space: The yellow background leaves a lot of empty space around the character, making the composition feel open.
  7. Overlap: The ears sit behind the head, while the head overlaps the shirt, creating a sense of layering.
  8. Color: The limited color palette and black outlines make each shape easy to recognize.

Reflection

Recreating Miffy helped me understand how simple shapes and positioning can create a recognizable image.

  1. Basic shapes: I used border-radius to turn rectangles into rounded shapes for the head, ears, and balloon.
  2. Layering: I used z-index to control which shapes appeared in front of or behind others.
  3. Proportions: Keeping the balloon and head around the same size helped the recreation look closer to the original.
  4. Alignment: Small details like connecting the string to the hand made the overall composition look more accurate.
  5. Spacing: I learned that empty space is just as important as the shapes themselves in keeping the design balanced.
